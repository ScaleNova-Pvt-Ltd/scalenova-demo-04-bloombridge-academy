# Live Client Demo Script — Bloombridge Academy

Use this walkthrough when pitching to ed-tech founders, university executive education deans, or executive coaching companies:

1. **Academic Authority & Visual Identity**:
   - Open `index.html`.
   - Point out the combination of academic prestige and frontier technology: Oxford Violet, Lavender subtle layers, and the dynamic interactive Skills & Competency Matrix canvas reacting to cursor hover.
   - Show how the site immediately establishes trust through verified statistics (₹48 LPA average alumni package, 1:12 faculty ratio, 140+ hiring partners).

2. **Program Catalog & Syllabus Depth**:
   - Navigate through `programs.html` and `program-detail.html`.
   - Show the 16-week modular breakdown, live capstone specs, and faculty credentials.
   - Show `career-development.html` and `success-stories.html` to demonstrate how ScaleNova structures the student value journey.

3. **High-Converting Candidate Application Funnel**:
   - Open `book-counselling.html`.
   - Walk through the structured admissions fields: Current Employer, Experience Bracket (e.g. 8-12 years), Corporate Sponsorship vs. Self-Funded, and Target Cohort.
   - Submit the application. Show the generated `SN-BLO-XXXXXX` candidate receipt modal.
   - Confirm that the admissions team receives the candidate directly in `Demo4_Education` and Frappe CRM.
