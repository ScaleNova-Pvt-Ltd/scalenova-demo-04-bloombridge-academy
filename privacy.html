Academic Governance

Student Record Privacy & Data Stewardship

Bloombridge Academy adheres to the highest standards of data governance under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and global credential standards.

1. Confidentiality of Candidate Data

All resumes, technical portfolios, employer references, and salary disclosures submitted during the admissions process are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share candidate profiles with hiring partners without explicit fellow consent.

2. Enterprise CRM Integration

Applications are processed securely through ScaleNova Enterprise CRM with zero third-party retargeting scripts or tracking pixels.
