Faculty Admissions Evaluation Dimensions

Candidates are scored across four dimensions during the 30-minute faculty dialogue:

Scoring Dimensions:
• Architectural Intuition (35%): Ability to articulate tradeoffs (latency vs. consistency, cloud costs vs. velocity)
• Production Pedigree (25%): Experience operating high-scale software with active users and SLA guarantees
• Socratic Contribution (25%): Willingness to actively challenge and constructively critique peer architectures
• Leadership Trajectory (15%): Clarity of intent regarding the Principal Engineer or VP/CTO career leap
